## 1. What OpenORDO is

OpenORDO is a multi-tenant, cloud-based clinic management system. Each clinic that signs up gets its own private workspace (a "**Clinic**"). Depending on your plan and add-ons, the Service lets you:

- manage **patients** (demographics, contact details, blood group, allergies, conditions);
- schedule **appointments** and manage **doctors** and working days;
- create **invoices** and track paid and unpaid balances;
- keep **medical records** (diagnosis, prescription text, clinical notes);
- invite **staff** with roles (Owner, Admin, Doctor, Front Desk);
- publish a **public booking page** at `/book/your-clinic`;
- buy optional **add-ons**: Video Consultation, E-Prescriptions, Inventory Management and Branded Booking Page.

OpenORDO is **software for clinics**. We are not a healthcare provider, hospital, pharmacy or telemedicine provider, and we do not practise medicine. See the [Medical Disclaimer](/legal/medical-disclaimer).

Add-ons are separate from plans and can be purchased by clinics on any plan, including Starter.

| Add-on | What it does |
